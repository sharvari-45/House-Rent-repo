## 1. Problem Background
Finding affordable, verified rental accommodation in urban Indian cities (such as Bangalore, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR) is notoriously challenging. Tenants frequently encounter:
* High, non-refundable brokerage fees charged by intermediaries (often 1 to 2 months' rent).
* Fake or outdated listings with misleading photos and fabricated amenity details.
* Lack of direct communication channels with verified property owners.
* Unclear booking statuses with no accountability.

Simultaneously, property owners face difficulty finding verified, reliable tenants, keeping track of incoming inquiries, and managing listing availability across disjointed platforms.

---

## 2. Problem Statement
> **"How might we create a centralized, transparent full-stack rental platform where prospective tenants can discover verified housing, interact directly with property owners without intermediaries, and track rental bookings through automated administrative moderation?"**

---

## 3. Target Audience & Stakeholders
1. **Tenants:** Working professionals, college students, and families looking for residential houses, flats, and studios.
2. **Property Owners (Landlords):** Homeowners and landlords wanting to list their residential properties, verify applications, and manage occupancy.
3. **Administrators:** Platform supervisors responsible for moderating property submissions, preventing fraudulent entries, and overseeing user records.

---

## 4. Key Goals & Success Metrics
* **Direct Connect:** 100% elimination of unauthorized brokerage commissions.
* **Listing Authenticity:** Every owner-submitted listing must be vetted and approved by an administrator before public broadcast.
* **Streamlined Booking:** End-to-end digital rental request workflow with transparent status updates (`Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`, `Cancelled`).
