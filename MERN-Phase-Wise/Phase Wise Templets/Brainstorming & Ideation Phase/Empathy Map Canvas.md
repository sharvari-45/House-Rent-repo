# Empathy Map Canvas — House Rent

## Persona 1: Rahul Verma (Tenant Persona)
* **Demographics:** 24-year-old Software Engineer relocating to Bangalore for employment.
* **Says:**
  * *"I need a clean 2BHK near tech parks without paying 2 months' brokerage."*
  * *"I want real photos and clear pricing without hidden maintenance charges."*
* **Thinks:**
  * *"Will the landlord respond promptly to my move-in inquiry?"*
  * *"Are the advertised amenities (WiFi, Power Backup) genuinely operational?"*
* **Does:**
  * Searches online real estate portals and filters by city, rent budget, and bedroom count.
  * Submits rental requests with intended move-in dates and personal introductions.
* **Pains:**
  * High upfront brokerage fees.
  * Misleading listing photos and unavailable properties marked as active.
* **Gains:**
  * Direct contact with verified hosts.
  * Real-time tracking of lease application status.

## Persona 2: Rajesh Sharma (Property Owner Persona)
* **Demographics:** 48-year-old homeowner with 2 apartments in Bangalore and Mumbai.
* **Says:**
  * *"I want reliable, background-checked tenants who treat the home with care."*
  * *"I need an easy way to mark my property as rented when occupied."*
* **Thinks:**
  * *"Is my listing reaching genuine working professionals?"*
  * *"How can I review tenant details before approving their visit?"*
* **Does:**
  * Uploads property specifications, amenities, and high-resolution photos.
  * Reviews incoming booking applications and either Approves or Rejects them with notes.
* **Pains:**
  * Random spam calls from local brokers.
  * Keeping listings updated manually.
* **Gains:**
  * Dedicated Landlord Dashboard with occupancy control and instant application notifications.
